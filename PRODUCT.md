# Deflectly — support AI trained on *your* product

**You paste your SaaS product's past support questions and answers. Deflectly trains a support bot on them in seconds — right in front of you. You watch it beat a generic bot on your own product questions. Then you put it live, and it keeps improving every night from new conversations.**

No setup project. No ML team. No "contact sales."

## The problem

Generic chatbots don't know that *your* Growth plan costs $49/user/month, that *your* SSO is SAML-only on Scale, or that *your* API rate limit is 2,000 requests/minute. They give vague, canned answers, customers get annoyed, and you end up answering the same tickets anyway.

But your support inbox already contains the right answers — your agents wrote them hundreds of times. That history is the training data. Deflectly uses it.

## How it works (takes about 3 minutes)

1. **Paste your tickets.** Copy Q/A pairs from Intercom, Help Scout, or Zendesk (or upload a file). Don't have them handy? Click "Load sample SaaS" to see it work with 60 example tickets from a fictional B2B workflow-automation platform.
2. **Click Train.** A live log shows the model being built on *your* tickets. It finishes in under a second.
3. **Watch the comparison.** Five brand-new test questions — questions the model never saw — go to both a generic bot and your freshly trained model. You see both answers side by side with real scores. In our sample run: the generic bot got **0 out of 5 right**; the model trained on your own tickets got **5 out of 5**.
4. **Pick a plan.** Starter $49/mo · Growth $99/mo · Scale $199/mo. (In this demo, clicking a plan just records your interest — nothing is charged.)

## Once you're live

- Your bot answers customers in your product's chat and email, using your docs and past answers — grounded in your tickets, never making things up.
- Every night it retrains on the newest conversations and answers, and only ships if it scores better than the current version. You get a short report.
- Tricky questions get handed to a human automatically.
- A SaaS doing 2,500 support conversations/month that deflects 60% of them saves roughly $3,000/month in support time. The Growth plan costs $99.

## What you need

Just your past support tickets. If you can copy-paste, you can train your own support model.

**Try it:** the demo runs at `http://localhost:8451` — paste tickets, train, watch it win, then pick a plan.
