Embed the support widget

Paste this snippet on your SaaS site. It adds a chat bubble that answers visitors using your trained Deflectly model.

Copy this into your site's <head>:
<script defer src="{{ request.host_url }}static/widget.js"
  data-endpoint="{{ request.host_url }}api/predict"
  data-title="Support"
  data-primary="#0a6e3c"></script>
The widget posts to /api/predict on your Deflectly instance, so it only works once you've trained a model (Step 3).
